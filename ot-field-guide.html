Using the OT Field Guide

One guide in five parts: Orient (how OT differs from IT), Respond (rules of engagement and a safety-gated triage tool), Investigate (nine forensic playbooks in priority order), Analyse (indicators, artefact locations, scenarios, case studies) and Reference (frameworks and training).

Working in the field
  • Click a step's number in any playbook to tick it off. Progress shows in the sidebar and is kept in this browser only.
  • The triage tool updates its decision as you answer. Copy handover puts a Markdown handover note on your clipboard for the OT engineering lead.
  • Search finds terms, protocols, assets and playbook steps. Press / to jump to search.
  • Commands have a copy button. Every path and command is typical, not guaranteed: confirm against the vendor, version and site before you rely on it.
Not engineering authority

This is decision support and an orientation aid for practitioners. It does not replace engineering judgement, operations authority, functional safety assessment or vendor guidance. Decisions about OT assets, especially anything connected to a safety instrumented system, belong to qualified engineering, operations and safety personnel at the site. Use at your own risk; no liability is accepted for loss, damage or injury arising from its use.
