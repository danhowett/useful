Mapping observed activity to ATT&CK

Built on MITRE ATT&CK Enterprise v19.2 (released 5 August 2026): 15 tactics, 222 techniques and 475 sub-techniques, including the April 2026 split of Defense Evasion into Stealth and Defense Impairment. Ransomware TTP profiles and indicators of compromise from ransomware.live (TTP data from crocodyli/ThreatActors-TTPs) are bundled, retrieved 12 October 2026. Everything runs locally in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

💾
Your mapping saves in this browser automatically. In Chrome or Edge, use Auto-save to file… for a copy on disk, or export JSON.
Workflow
  1. Add observations: tools, commands, event IDs, behaviours or technique IDs. Paste a block of notes to add one observation per line.
  2. Review suggestions (amber) in the matrix. Click a technique to put it in scope with a confidence level, time and evidence, or rule it out with a reason.
  3. Compare against ATT&CK groups, software, campaigns and ransomware.live ransomware profiles, and see which mitigations and detection strategies cover the in-scope techniques.
  4. Check IoCs: hashes, IPs, domains, emails, wallets and Tox IDs in your notes are matched against ransomware.live indicators. Use the IoC lookup page for ad hoc checks.
  5. Export the report, a Navigator layer, CSV or Markdown.
Good to know
  • Suggestions are keyword-based starting points, not conclusions. Only techniques you put in scope appear as findings.
  • Profile overlap shows shared behaviour, not attribution. Many actors use the same common techniques.
  • IoC matches are leads, not proof. Hosting-provider IPs are reused, affiliates move between brands, and indicators age; confirm against your own telemetry and current intelligence.
  • Navigator layers and older IDs are upgraded on import; revoked techniques are mapped to their replacements.
