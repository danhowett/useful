Naming the adversary

A searchable reference of 68 threat-actor groups across nation-state espionage and disruption, financial crime, hacktivism and mixed motivation. The same actor is tracked under several names; search by any of them, filter in the sidebar, and copy a formatted brief for one actor or a selected set.

Reference only — verify with current advisories. Actor naming, attribution and operational status change continuously, and major groups are tracked under different names by different vendors. Do not rely on this index as a live intelligence source without checking NCSC, CISA, MITRE ATT&CK and primary vendor reporting. Selection as of May 2026.

Using the index

Search by actor name, alias, sponsor or attribution in the sidebar, and narrow by motivation, attribution and status. Each card gives a three-sentence orientation plus the key metadata.

⧉
Copying a brief. Use copy on any card for that actor, or tick several cards and use Copy intel brief to get a combined, formatted brief on the clipboard — handy for a ticket, threat note or email. Select shown ticks everything matching the current filters.
Reading a card
  • Status — Active, Disrupted, Historical or Dormant, as last assessed.
  • AKA — the group's other vendor names; all are searchable.
  • Attribution and sponsor sit in the metadata line, with the MITRE ATT&CK group ID where one exists.
