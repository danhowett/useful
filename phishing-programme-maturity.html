Getting started

Assesses a phishing programme across six dimensions and 35 controls, each with four described tiers. Covers the people side (simulations, learning, culture) and the technical and response layers around it, aligned to the NCSC's four-layer approach. Everything runs locally in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

💾
Turn on auto-save to file. Work is saved in this browser automatically, but that copy is lost if browsing data is cleared. In Chrome or Edge, use Auto-save to file… in the sidebar. In other browsers, export JSON regularly.
Workflow
  1. Setup: choose sector and size. These set the expected tier for each control.
  2. Score each control by choosing the tier description that best matches reality. An accurate L1 is more useful than an aspirational L3.
  3. Record confidence and evidence. High = tested or observed; Medium = documents reviewed; Low = interview only.
  4. Review anti-patterns: practices that may be doing harm. They are prioritised above tier gaps.
  5. Assign owners and dates in the improvement plan, then generate the report.
Rules
  • Not applicable needs a justification; Not assessed is excluded from calculations and listed as a limitation.
  • Dimension scores are the mean of scored controls. The programme tier is set by the weakest dimension.
  • Action priority = gap to target × likelihood × impact, with anti-patterns ranked first.
Exporting the report

Open Generate report, choose the full report or the board summary, then Download PDF. In the print dialog choose Save as PDF, margins Default, Background graphics on and Headers and footers off.
