Getting started

An organisation-wide maturity assessment against NIST CSF 2.0, ISO/IEC 27001:2022 or CIS Controls v8.1, scored on a 0–5 scale aligned to ISO/IEC 33020 capability levels. Everything runs locally in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

💾
Turn on auto-save to file. Work is saved in this browser automatically, but that copy is lost if browsing data is cleared. In Chrome or Edge, use Auto-save to file… in the sidebar. In other browsers, export JSON regularly.
Workflow
  1. Setup: choose the framework, record scope and team, and apply a target preset.
  2. Score each area 0–5 using the level descriptors and the "Level 3 looks like" anchor. Levels are cumulative: award a level only when every lower level is met.
  3. Record confidence and evidence. High = tested or observed; Medium = documents reviewed; Low = interview only.
  4. Review the dashboard, gap & risk view, roadmap, incident readiness and CAF alignment, then generate the report.
Rules
  • Not applicable requires a justification and appears in the report's register.
  • Not assessed is excluded from all calculations and listed as a limitation.
  • Priority = gap to target × likelihood × impact. Each area has default likelihood, impact and effort values you can adjust.
  • The CAF alignment view is indicative. For a formal CAF assessment use the dedicated CAF tool.
Exporting the report

Open Generate report, then Download PDF. In the print dialog choose Save as PDF, margins Default, Background graphics on and Headers and footers off.

Framework names and references are used for alignment only. This tool supports professional judgement and is not a certification or regulatory determination.
