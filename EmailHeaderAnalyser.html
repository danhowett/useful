Analysing an email

Paste the full message source or drop an .eml file. The analyser compares who the message claims to be from with who actually sent it, reads the receiving server's SPF, DKIM, DMARC and ARC results, traces the delivery path with timing, and pulls out indicators, links and attachment hashes.

🔒
Nothing leaves this page. There are no network requests: no fonts, lookups or analytics. Message content is kept in memory only and is cleared when you close the tab or press Clear.
Getting the source
  • Outlook (new and web): open the message › … › View › View message source.
  • Outlook (classic): File › Properties › Internet headers (headers only), or save as .eml via drag and drop.
  • Gmail: ⋮ › Show original › Download original.
  • Microsoft 365 investigations: export from Explorer or eDiscovery as .eml.
Reading the results
  • Only the topmost Authentication-Results header from the receiving organisation is trusted; headers lower down can be forged by the sender.
  • Passing DMARC proves the From domain, not the sender's intent. Compromised accounts and business email compromise often pass.
  • Delivery hops are read bottom (oldest) to top (newest). Negative gaps between hops point to clock skew or forged headers.
